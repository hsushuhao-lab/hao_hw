## Current milestone — M0 Environment Walkthrough

Goal: prove the continuous space before implementing cooking.

Playable flow:
- move through a realistic consultation room;
- cross a prep/transition zone;
- enter the rear kitchen;
- interact with clinic and kitchen stations.

Controls: WASD / Arrow keys, Shift to walk faster, E interact, R reset.
